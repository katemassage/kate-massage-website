Payment Policy

50% deposit: A deposit equal to half of the service price is required in advance to reserve an appointment.

Remaining balance: The other half is due immediately after the service.

An appointment is not confirmed until the deposit has been received and acknowledged.

Please contact us first to confirm accepted payment methods and receive payment instructions. Do not send payment before receiving confirmation.

Example: For a $200 service, the deposit is $100 and the remaining balance is $100.

Helpful Information

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment?

Complete the appointment enquiry form or contact us directly. Your appointment must be confirmed with us before it is reserved.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. A 50% deposit is required in advance to reserve an appointment. The remaining balance is due immediately after the service.

How do I make a payment?

Contact us first to confirm accepted payment methods and receive payment instructions. Do not send payment before confirmation.

Can I ask questions before booking?

Absolutely. You are welcome to contact us with questions about services, pricing, appointment arrangements and suitability.

Will my appointment be confirmed immediately?

No. Sending an enquiry does not automatically confirm an appointment. We will communicate with you to confirm availability and the deposit.
