Payment & Reservation Policy

01. Reservation Deposit — 50%

A deposit equal to 50% of your selected service price is required in advance to reserve your appointment.

02. Remaining Balance — 50%

The remaining 50% of the service price is due immediately after your appointment has been completed.

03. Appointment Confirmation

Sending an appointment request does not automatically confirm your booking. Your appointment is confirmed only after your deposit has been received and acknowledged by Kate Massage.

04. Payment Arrangements

Please contact us before making any payment to confirm the accepted payment methods and payment instructions.

Example: $200 Appointment

Advance deposit: $100

Remaining balance after service: $100
